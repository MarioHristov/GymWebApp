Frequently asked questions

Answers about visits, classes and membership.

What should I bring?

Bring clean indoor shoes, water and a towel.

Can children visit?

Visitors under 14 should come with an adult.

How do I reserve a place?

Sign in, choose a session on the schedule and reserve it. You can cancel through your profile before the session starts.

What does my membership include?

Standard includes gym access. Premium and Diamond include group classes. Personal training uses a coaching pack.
