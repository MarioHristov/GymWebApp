Member information

How enrollment, reservations and account records work.

Enrollment

Confirming a plan records the enrollment and the amount payable at the club. Existing club membership periods run consecutively.

Reservations

Group sessions require Premium or Diamond coverage on the class date. Coaching uses one pack credit per session. Cancel before the start time to release the place and credit.
